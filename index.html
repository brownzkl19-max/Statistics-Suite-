📚 Learn Statistics

Mean

The mean is the average of a dataset. Add all values then divide by the number of values.


Example: 1,2,3,4,5
Mean = 3


Median

The median is the middle value after the data has been sorted.

Example: 1,2,3,4,5 Median = 3

Mode

The mode is the value that appears most often.

Example: 1,1,2,3,4 Mode = 1


Q1 (First Quartile)

Q1 is the value below which 25% of the data falls.


Q2 (Median)

Q2 is the middle point of the dataset. 50% of values lie below it.


Q3 (Third Quartile)

Q3 is the value below which 75% of the data falls.


IQR

IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of data.

Formula: IQR = Q3 − Q1


Z-Score

Z-Score shows how many standard deviations a value is from the mean.

Positive = Above average Negative = Below average


Percentile

A percentile tells you the percentage of data that falls below a value.

P90 means a value is higher than 90% of the dataset.


Outliers

Outliers are unusual values that are much higher or lower than the rest of the data.


Correlation

Correlation measures the strength of the relationship between two variables.

r = 1 → Perfect Positive r = 0 → No Relationship r = -1 → Perfect Negative


Regression

Regression predicts a value using another variable.
